The real problem is the timing Revenue is settled at signature. Results are not.

PEPM is invoiced from month one, calculated on everyone eligible, and collected before a single employee has opened the product. The vendor's year is complete at the point of sale. Your client's return, if there is one, arrives much later or never. Nothing in the structure requires the two to ever meet — which is how a vendor posts a strong year in the same period its buyers' confidence falls thirty points. Change when the money moves and the problem stops existing.

Objections, answered

The questions brokers actually ask.

Straight answers. Bring the hard ones to the call and get them on the record.

If it's free to everyone, how does AssetAlign make money?

On the assets and services employees voluntarily choose — advisory relationships, insurance, tax, estate and mortgage services accessed through the network. Roughly 1.00% of managed assets flows as platform revenue, AssetAlign's share is 37.5% of that, and a defined portion of its share is returned to the employer's Flex Fund.

Nothing is charged upfront, which means AssetAlign only does well if employees genuinely engage. That alignment is the point.

What's the catch for my client?

There's no fee, no plan amendment and no ongoing administration. The realistic asks are a census file and a decision to make the benefit available. Communication, onboarding, scheduling and support are all handled by AssetAlign.

Does this compete with the 401(k) advisor, or with my firm's own wealth arm?

It's built to sit alongside what's already there. It works with the existing 401(k), EAP and health benefits and ties them into one financial wellness story rather than replacing any of them. Because there's no cost, it doesn't compete for budget with your other lines either.

If your firm already has a wealth or financial-planning practice, this is usually the piece that has been missing from it rather than a rival to it. Account minimums mean that practice can only serve the top of a client's census; the other eighty percent were never economically reachable. AssetAlign covers that population at no cost to anyone, gives every one of them a plan and a fiduciary, and grows their assets over time — which is exactly how people cross a minimum. Whether your own advisors sit inside the network is a conversation worth having on the first call.

How am I compensated?

AssetAlign shares a portion of platform revenue with the broker or consultant who brings the relationship in — the same mechanism that funds the employer Flex Fund and employee rewards. The specific rate is confirmed on the intro call. Ask early so you can model it accurately in the calculator.

Are the advisors actually fiduciaries?

Yes — independent fiduciary advisors, vetted before they enter the network, operating nationally. Each employee sees the full profile before booking: bio, credentials, specialties, client stats and a direct scheduling link.

What about ERISA and fiduciary exposure for my client?

AssetAlign is built to sit outside your client's ERISA plans rather than inside them: participation is voluntary and employee-elected, the employer contributes nothing, and the employer's role is limited to making the benefit available and letting AssetAlign communicate it. That is the same facilitation-only posture the Department of Labor describes for third-party workplace programs (Technical Release 2026-02), and AssetAlign's position is that the arrangement falls within the voluntary-program safe harbor at 29 C.F.R. § 2510.3-1(j), so no plan amendments are needed and HR takes on no plan-fiduciary role.

Two things to say plainly to a client. First, the employer should not endorse or vouch for the advisors; making the benefit available and sharing AssetAlign's materials is fine, a stamp of approval is not. Second, because the Flex Fund returns value to the employer, the client's own ERISA counsel should review the arrangement before launch. AssetAlign will provide its analysis and answer counsel's questions directly.

Is participation mandatory for employees?

No. Every employee is eligible and every employee has a plan made available to them, but working with an advisor is 100% voluntary. Employees who engage are the ones who earn credits.

What size client does this work for?

Because there's no cost and no integration requirement, it scales down further than most wellness vendors. The rewards and Flex Fund grow with participation and assets, so larger and more engaged groups build faster — but the barrier to starting is the same at any size.
